The shield, with an F where FLUT won't fit

Below 48px the full mark turns to mush, so the tab icons carry the F and the home-screen icon carries all four letters.

src/app/favicon.ico · replaces today's

16, 32 and 48 in one ICO. The 16 is hand-hinted: the F sits on whole pixels, with a 2px stem and bars. The 32 and 48 come from icon.svg. The 16 is shown at 6× on the right.

src/app/icon.svg · new

The app's own shield path (.clip-pennant) in brass #e0ac4a, with the F in #1c150a. Next emits it as <link rel="icon" type="image/svg+xml">, and modern browsers prefer it over the ICO.

src/app/apple-icon.png · new · 180 × 180

Full bleed, no transparency: iOS rounds the corners itself (right). Board vignette ground, a 128 × 140 shield, and FLUT in Archivo 900 at 30px with −0.6px tracking.
