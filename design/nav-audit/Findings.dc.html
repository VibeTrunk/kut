Read against app-nav.tsx, nav-items.tsx, lib/nav/context.ts, the (app) route tree and globals.css. Two of these are yours — both hold. Several are cosmetic and one is a genuine layout bug. Nothing here is a rewrite; the most expensive item is a redirect and a re-point of two tabs.

A — Structure and wayfinding

where things live, and whether you can tell where you are
01

Nine of fifteen destinations light nothing up

high

Active styling is computed only for the five primaryNavItems. Stand on the leaderboard, the Chronicle, Messages, the player directory, a player profile, Settings, Club Value, Trade offers or any admin page, and nothing in the chrome is highlighted — the “More” button has no active state of its own, and neither does the mobile hamburger. For most of the app there is no “you are here” at all.

Fix: every destination should map to something that can light up. That is most of the argument for the restructure — but even without it, giving “More” an active state when the current route is one of its children is a five-line change.

app-nav.tsx:118, 158 · nav-items.tsx:29–36

02

Three of five primary tabs are the same branch — and the branch’s hub is the emptiest page in the app

high

Collection, Packs and Club all sit under /club. What /club itself does: repeat four stats you can see elsewhere, offer two big cards linking to Collection and Packs (already one tap away in the same bar), and close with “Squad building is planned for a future season.” It is a hub whose children have all outgrown it.

On a phone this is worse than on desktop: three of five thumb slots point into one branch, and one of them is a page with nothing on it.

Fix: retire /club with a redirect to /club/collection, exactly as /sessions already redirects to /chronicle. Its Club Value figure and card counts move onto Collection’s header, which already computes most of them.

club/page.tsx:1–80 · nav-items.tsx:29–36 · sessions/page.tsx

03

“More” is four unrelated jobs in one unlabelled list

high

Club-wide reading (Leaderboard, Chronicle, Player directory), your own economy (Club Value, Trade offers), your mail (Messages), help (How KUT works), and account (Settings, Admin, Log out). Nine rows, one divider — above Log out — and no ordering anyone could reconstruct. A member looking for the leaderboard has to open a menu named after the absence of a category.

Fix: a menu called “More” should hold one kind of thing. Keep the account group, promote the content.

nav-items.tsx:38–51

04

Messages is the app’s notification centre and it is the eighth row of a dropdown

high

It is where attendance rewards, the bibs bonus, pack opens, sales, purchases, trade offers, trade responses and admin notices land — the only surface that tells a member something happened to them, and the only one with a real unread count. It is filed below the player directory.

Fix: its own control in the bar with the count on it, on both platforms.

messages/page.tsx:20–38 · nav-items.tsx:45

05

Club Value holds a permanent nav slot it does not need

agreed — remove

Four in-context routes already exist: the Home stat tile, the /club stat tile, the /club prose link, and the leaderboard’s own header paragraph. The menu row is the fifth, the least contextual, and the only one you pay a permanent slot for. Your read is right.

page.tsx:127 · club/page.tsx:41,63 · leaderboard/page.tsx:56

06

Trade offers likewise — but it is currently carrying the only offer count in the app

agreed, with a condition

Also right: the Market header button and the How-KUT-works page both lead there, and every incoming offer already writes you a trade_offer notification. Three routes remain without the menu row.

The condition: that row’s badgeCount is fed by incomingOfferCount, and the button that replaces it on /market renders no count at all. Delete the row as it stands and the number disappears from the product.

Fix: move the count onto the Market tab and onto an Offers tab inside Market. getNavContext already queries it — nothing new to fetch.

nav-items.tsx:44 · lib/nav/context.ts:36–42 · market/page.tsx:85

07

One event, two counters, and then one anonymous dot

medium-high

An incoming trade offer increments incomingOfferCount and inserts an unread trade_offer notification. Two badges, two menu rows, one event. Both then collapse on the closed control into a single 6px brass dot (moreHasUnread) that says something happened but never what — and on a phone that dot is the only signal on the screen.

Fix: one badge per thing. Offers count on Market; unread messages count on the messages control. Drop the merged dot.

app-nav.tsx:110, 145, 166

08

Home and the Chronicle answer the same question at two addresses

medium

Home’s headline is “This week in KUT”. The Chronicle is “one issue per football week”. Both are the weekly report; they were built at different times and never introduced to each other. Home links to /chronicle only through the description text of a session row in the activity feed.

Fix: the latest issue becomes Home’s lead block, with “back issues” as the route to the index. That is also what frees the fifth tab.

page.tsx:97,192 · chronicle/page.tsx

B — Chrome and interaction

the bar itself, and the phone
09

Mobile runs two primary navigations, pointing in opposite directions

high

A thumb-reachable bottom bar carries five tabs, one of which is redundant (02). Everything else — nine destinations including your mail — lives behind an icon-only hamburger in the top-right, the hardest corner of a 390×844 screen to reach one-handed.

Fix: nothing high-frequency should live up there. Messages and account are legitimate top-right residents because each is a single known destination; a drawer holding the other half of the app is not.

app-nav.tsx:139–196

10

The disc that looks like an account button is decorative

medium

36px, brass, your initials — and aria-hidden="true", no link, no handler. The real account menu is its neighbour, labelled “More”, whose panel opens headed by your display name. The two controls have swapped jobs, and the inert one is the one people will press.

Fix: make the avatar the trigger and drop the word “More”.

app-nav.tsx:170

11

The desktop dropdown is reused verbatim as the mobile menu

medium

The same MoreMenu component, absolute right-0 top-full mt-2 w-64, anchored to a 36px button in a 56px header. The touch targets are right (min-h-11) and dismissal is handled, so this is not broken — it is a pointer pattern on a touch device: no sheet, no drag-to-dismiss, no reachability.

Fix: a bottom sheet on mobile, the dropdown on desktop. One of the divergences worth adding.

app-nav.tsx:24, 173

12

Content can be clipped behind the mobile tab bar

medium — a real bug

The tab bar is ~60px of its own content plus pb-[env(safe-area-inset-bottom)]. The content wrapper reserves a flat pb-16 = 64px. On a phone with a 34px home indicator the bar is ~94px tall against 64px of reserved space, so the last ~30px of every page sits underneath it — including the last row of the market grid and the Log out button at the foot of Settings.

Fix: padding-bottom: calc(4rem + env(safe-area-inset-bottom)) on the wrapper, below sm only.

app-nav.tsx:181 · (app)/layout.tsx:12

13

role="menu" promises keyboard behaviour the menu does not implement

medium

The panel sets role="menu" with role="menuitem" links, which tells a screen reader to expect arrow-key navigation, a roving tabindex and focus return on Escape. None of those exist — Escape closes the panel but focus is not restored to the trigger. The primary nav sets aria-current correctly; the menu never does, so an assistive user gets less wayfinding there, not more.

Fix: either implement the pattern, or drop the roles and let it be a labelled list of links — which is what it actually is. Add aria-current either way.

app-nav.tsx:22, 40, 43

C — Consistency

same thing, different clothes
14

Three grids of the same cards, three filter vocabularies

medium-high

Market and Player directory: a <form> of selects with an explicit Filter submit. Collection → Manage: instant-navigation chips for tier, a link row for sort, and a search box with no button. Collection → Album: a third vocabulary again — “Lens”, with All / Gaps / Specialists / By type / By tier. Search, tier and sort mean the same thing in all three places.

The code comment on Collection says it adopted “the same search / tier / sort vocabulary the Market always had”. It adopted the same fields, not the same controls.

Fix: one filter bar component. Chips for a short enumerated set (tier), a select for a long one (archetype), search always in the same slot, sort always at the right end. Album’s lens stays distinct — it is genuinely a different idea — but should stop borrowing tier and type, which the shared bar already does.

market/page.tsx:91–133 · players/page.tsx:66–108 · club/collection/page.tsx:157–200 · album/collection-album.tsx

15

Filters apply on submit in two places and on click in the third

medium

Tap a tier chip in your Collection and the grid changes. Choose a tier on the Market and nothing happens until you find the Filter button. Same user, same session, same mental model, two outcomes.

Fix: pick one. Instant is better on a phone (no second reach), and the Market’s price bounds are the only control that genuinely wants a submit — give those an explicit Apply inside the filter sheet.

16

One section, three names

medium

The tab says Market, the page kicker says Transfer market, the h1 says Buy Live Cards, and the back link from a listing says ← Transfer market. The same split runs elsewhere: nav Chronicle / h1 KUT Chronicle; nav Collection / kicker My club; nav Club Value / kicker My club.

Fix: one name per section, used in the tab, the h1 and the back link. Keep the kicker for the editorial line above it — that is what a kicker is for — but stop letting it name the section.

market/page.tsx:76,79 · market/[listingId]/page.tsx:118 · chronicle/page.tsx

D — Mobile in particular

beyond the navigation itself
21

The mobile page header is the desktop header at desktop size

medium

Every page opens with a kicker, a 48px serif h1 and a two-to-three-line description. On the Market that is followed by a Trade-offers button and the filter block, and the first listing starts 597px down a 844px screen — you see the top three-quarters of one card. KB-008 already cut the filter block from 352px to 232px; the header itself was never looked at.

Fix: below sm, h1 to ~30px and drop the description on utility pages (Market, Collection, Leaderboard, Packs). Keep it on editorial ones (Chronicle, How KUT works, Club Value) where the prose is the page. Same change moves the first listing to ~259px and puts four cards on screen.

On a detail page the primary action is far below the fold

medium

Card detail and listing detail both stack a detail-size card — 330px wide, ~460px tall — above the name, the attribute bars, a rule, a stats table and only then Buy, List or Discard. On desktop the two-column grid puts the action beside the card, so this is a purely mobile problem.

Fix: a sticky action bar above the tab bar below sm, carrying the one primary action and its price. Desktop unchanged.

23

Home’s activity ledger becomes three rows per entry on a phone

low

The ledger is a three-track grid from sm up and stacks below it: kind, description, timestamp, each on its own line, twelve times. It is the last block on the most-visited page and it is the longest.

Fix: below sm, put the kicker and time on one line and the description under it — two rows, not three — and cap at six with a “See all” link.

page.tsx:196–214

If you only do five things

Ordered by what a member would feel first, not by what is cheapest.

  1. Give every destination a “you are here”. Either restructure, or — as a same-day stopgap — light “More” when the current route is one of its children.
  2. Pull Messages out of the menu and give it its own control with a real number. It is the app talking to the member and it is currently whispering.
  3. Remove Club Value and Trade offers from the menu, exactly as you said — and move the offer count onto Market in the same commit, so no signal is lost.
  4. Retire /club with a redirect, and give the freed tab to the leaderboard. Three of five tabs stop pointing at one branch.
  5. Fix the safe-area padding. Two lines, and it stops eating the bottom of every page on a modern iPhone.

What needs an ADR: retiring /club and re-pointing the primary tabs is a navigation change of the kind Phase B recorded, so it wants a decision entry and a BUILD_SPEC touch. Nothing here changes a game rule, an economy formula or a Part L invariant — no migration, no schema work. The offer badge moves in the UI only; getNavContext already fetches the number it needs.
