one you

The junk drawer goes away entirely. Every destination becomes one of five tabs, an in-page tab beneath one of them, the messages button, or the account menu — and every one of them can then show “you are here”, which nine of today’s cannot.

The two controls on the right finally do what they look like

Today that brass disc is decorative — aria-hidden, no link, no handler — while the real account menu sits beside it labelled “More” and opens headed by your name. Two neighbours: the one that looks like an account button is inert, the one that is the account menu is called something else.

Here the disc is the menu, and holds only account things. The speech bubble beside it is Messages with a real number on it, so an unread attendance reward and an incoming trade offer stop collapsing into one anonymous 6px dot that tells you something happened but never what.

Everything else moved into the tabs. Nothing stays filed under a word that means “the rest”.

What moved, and why

the nine-item More menu, dismantled
Was in “More”NowReasoning
Club Value/club/value In context only — nothing in the chrome Already reachable four ways: the Home stat tile, the /club stat tile, the /club prose link and the leaderboard’s own header paragraph. A fifth, least contextual entry earns nothing. Your call, and it holds.
Trade offers/market/offers A tab inside Market — and the badge goes with it Agreed, with one condition. That menu row is today the only place the incoming-offer count appears, and the in-context entry — the “Trade offers” button on /market — carries no count at all. Move the number onto the Market tab and the Offers tab, or deleting the row quietly costs you the signal.
Messages/messages Its own button in the bar, with the number on it It is the notification centre of the entire app, and it is filed eighth in a dropdown.
Leaderboard/leaderboard A primary tab The competitive spine of a collecting game, and the destination Home’s Rank tile already points at.
Player directory/players A tab inside Leaderboard Both are rankings — one of clubs, one of players. Home and every empty album slot already link into it.
Chronicle/chronicle The lead block on Home; back issues one tap on Home’s headline is literally “This week in KUT” and the Chronicle is “one issue per football week”. Two addresses answering the same question — and Home never links to the current issue except through an activity row.
How KUT works · Settings · Admin · Log out The account menu The only four that genuinely belonged together, and now the only four left.
Club/club — a primary tab today Retires, redirecting to Collection Its whole job is linking to Collection and Packs — both already one tap away — plus Club Value, and it closes on a “squad building is planned” placeholder. Redirect it the way /sessions already redirects to /chronicle.
15 member destinations, none deeper than 2 taps every destination can show “you are here” one event, one badge, one place needs an ADR: /club retires, tabs re-point

Not drawn, deliberately. A third “My listings” tab under Market is the obvious next one — today a listing is cancelled from its card in the Collection, which is a reasonable place for it but leaves no way to see everything you currently have up for sale. That is a decision, not an assumption, so it is a question rather than a mockup.
