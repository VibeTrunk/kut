KUT navigation audit · consistency

Same thing,
different clothes

Three grids show the same cards, filtered by the same three fields, using three different sets of controls that behave two different ways. And one section carries three names depending on where you meet it.

What stays per-surface: the Market’s min/max price pair and the directory’s archetype select are genuinely local — they join the same bar, they do not change its grammar. The Album’s Lens is a different idea and should stay one, but it should stop carrying its own By type / By tier menus once the shared bar already offers them. And on a phone the whole bar collapses to one Filters pill plus chips for whatever is active — the sheet is where the price pair gets its Apply button, the one place a submit is genuinely wanted.

The kicker is not the problem — using it as a second name is. “Transfer market”, “KUT roster” and “KUT inbox” are good editorial lines and they should stay; they are what gives the app its clubblad voice. What should change is the h1 underneath: it is the one string a member will read as “where am I”, and it should be the same word that is lit in the tab bar. “Buy Live Cards” is the odd one out — it names an action, not a place, and there is now a whole Offers tab under it that is not buying.
