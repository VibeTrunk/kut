1

Nine of fifteen destinations light nothing up

Active styling is computed for the five primary items only. Stand on /leaderboard, /chronicle, /messages, /players, /settings, /club/value, /market/offers or any admin page and no part of the chrome is highlighted — the “More” button never takes a state of its own. For most of the app, there is no “you are here”.

2

The disc that looks like an account button isn’t one

36px, brass, your initials — and aria-hidden="true", no link, no handler. The actual account menu is its neighbour labelled “More”, whose panel opens headed by your display name. The two controls have swapped jobs.

3

Three of the five tabs are the same branch

Collection, Packs and Club all live under /club, and /club exists mostly to link to the first two. On a phone that spends three of five thumb slots on one branch.

4

One menu, four unrelated jobs, no grouping

Club-wide content (Leaderboard, Chronicle, Player directory), personal state (Club Value, Trade offers, Messages), help, and account — nine rows, one divider, filed in no order anyone could reconstruct. Weekly-use content sits next to Log out.

5

Two badges collapse into one anonymous dot

Unread messages and incoming trade offers each carry their own number inside the panel — but on the closed button they become a single 6px brass dot. It says something happened. It never says what, and on mobile that dot is the only signal on the whole screen.

Trade offers is the same call, with one string attached. The listing detail page and the Market header both lead there, and every incoming offer already writes you a trade_offer message. Three routes without the menu.

But that menu row carries the only incoming-offer count in the app, and the button on /market that replaces it carries no number at all. One event currently increments two separate counters — incomingOfferCount and an unread notification — which then merge into one dot. Delete the row, keep the number: put it on the Market tab.

Worth saying plainly: none of this is sloppiness. The nav was overhauled deliberately (Phase B, shipped) and the details are good — min-h-11 targets, visible focus rings, tabular coin figures, a badge that hides itself at zero. What it never got was a second pass on the shape once Chronicle, Trade offers, Club Value and the album had all been added on top of it. That is what this canvas is.
