# Midweek Madness — archetype rotation report

Generated by `node scripts/midweek/rotation.mjs` (`tests/sim/midweek-rotation.sim.ts`). **Do not edit by hand:** a full run rewrites this file. It is the C0 harness report of MM 2.0 for the weekly rotation of unclaimed Players' archetypes (ROADMAP, owner decisions 2026-09-30): the evidence for checkpoints Q8 (smooth the keeper count?) and Q9 (a rotating archetype shows a Player is unclaimed). It decides neither. The engine and its tuning are unchanged; only the simulated club rotates.

Run: every variant over the same 5,000 seasons × 20 weeks, seed 20260925, 2240 s in all. Collections, habits, injuries and the week's dice are identical across variants; only archetypes differ.

## The simulated club and the rotation

- The tuning club (`docs/archive/MIDWEEK_TUNING.md`): 30 Players, 22 members. Its seven specialists (two Goalkeepers, one each of Speedster, Finisher, Playmaker, Defender, Tank) are the **claimed** Players: their members chose the archetype, and claiming ends the rotation. The other 23 are unclaimed All-rounders, about 80% of the roster, and every one of them is active and collectible, so all of them rotate.
- The rotation runs before each week opens and the week plays what it set, as the ADR-099 snapshot freezes it. It is seeded and deterministic (a hash of seed, season, rotation period and Player), and a card always carries its Player's archetype of the week.
- The "thought-through" manager sees the week's archetypes, as the picker does, and fields its best Goalkeeper if it owns one. The fixed habits ignore archetypes.

| Variant | Rotation |
|---|---:|
| `off` | No rotation (today) |
| `uniform` | Weekly, each rotating Player draws any of the seven archetypes (the roadmap decision) |
| `deal` | Weekly, the seven archetypes dealt out evenly over the rotating Players |
| `quota3` | Weekly, exactly three rotating Goalkeepers, the rest drawn from the other six |
| `quota1` | Weekly, exactly one rotating Goalkeeper, the rest drawn from the other six |
| `every4` | Every four weeks, each rotating Player draws any of the seven archetypes |
